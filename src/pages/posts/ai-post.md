*Just to preface this, I do not hate AI. I think it is incredibly useful, when used correctly. But the current trajectory that we are on in terms of **how** we use AI is concerning to say the least. I myself use AI when appropriate, such as when I need to search for something oddly specific. So, please don't take this article as an anti-AI trash opinion. This is the opinion of someone who is halfways against AI, but not for the usual reasons.*


## An Opinion On Empathy and AI

*What happens to humanity when AI is able to do everything that we used to do for each other?* Wouldn't that make it so that showing love and affection towards each other is useless? What happens when one person sends an AI-generated love letter to their significant other, but that special someone uses an AI to read it and then generates a reply? Is that really love?

I would say no. It is not.

It sounds dystopian and horrible. It's quite literally a nightmare of mine. If no one takes the time to show up for others, then why are we even here? We would just be barnacles attached to the raft that is Earth, leeching away its beauty until our inevitable decay.

Humans need connection to be truly human. We thrive on empathy, love, and caring for each other. I am terrified by the thought of losing that emotional connection.

I believe that it will happen. I think that at some point in the future, AI will be advanced enough that we won't need to show up for each other anymore. No matter how we might deny it out of need to preserve our mental health, it is happening. And it _is_ depressing, there's no denying that.

Programming? It can be done by AI, at one hundred times the speed, albeit with less perfect results. Same for art, music, gaming, writing, and virtually every creative thing that humanity has made for itself.

We won't have anything left to do for each other. I could hand-draw a beautiful portrait of my friend, only for her boyfriend to generate a virtually identical image in less than a minute. My friend might appreciate the idea of the portrait, but she may not understand _why_ I chose to make it by hand instead of generating it quickly, like her boyfriend did.

The gift, though heartfelt on my part, would seem like needless work to her.

But despite this road that humanity seems to be taking, I believe that there is a light at the end of the tunnel. Partly I believe this because if I don't, I think depression might seize me. But I also believe this because I know that not all of us are sucked in by the allure of AI.

I think that those of us who are not can help those who are 'wake up.'

When we finally realize that AI has replaced the empathy in us with nothing, that we are just beings living on the Earth like the people in _Scythe_, by Neal Shusterman, that will be the moment we finally wake up from this delusion of AI grandeur.

It may take a long time, but it will happen. I truly believe it will.

But it has to start with us. So call that friend, or open that book. Purposefully work your way through a problem without asking AI. Compliment a stranger on the street.
